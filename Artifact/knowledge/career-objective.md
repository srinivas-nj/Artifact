# Career Objective

A career objective should be brief and role-specific. It helps recruiters understand your direction and fit.

## Good examples
- "Motivated B.Tech Computer Science student with a strong foundation in Java, Python, and web development, seeking an entry-level software engineering role to contribute to product development and learning."
- "Aspiring software developer with hands-on experience in full-stack web development and problem solving, aiming to build scalable user-focused applications in a growth-oriented team."

## Guidance
- Mention the target role and the value you can offer.
- Include key technical strengths relevant to the job.
- Keep it to 2 to 3 lines.
- For freshers, focus on learning ability, projects, and technical skills.
- Avoid generic phrases like hardworking professional seeking a challenging opportunity.
