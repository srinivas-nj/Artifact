# Resume Review

A resume should be reviewed for clarity, grammar, accuracy, and relevance.

## Quick review checklist
- Does the resume match the target role and job description?
- Are there spelling or grammar mistakes?
- Are the bullet points action-oriented?
- Are the top skills and projects relevant to the job?
- Are the achievements measurable and explicit?
- Is the resume easy to scan in under 30 seconds?

## Common issues to fix
- Generic phrases that do not show value
- Long paragraphs instead of concise bullet points
- Repeated skills without evidence
- Weak responsibilities without measurable outcomes
- Missing keywords from the target role

## Good language patterns
- Developed, implemented, designed, optimized, automated, improved, collaborated, analyzed, delivered.
