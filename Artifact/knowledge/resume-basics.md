# Resume Basics

A resume is a short, targeted document that tells recruiters why you are a strong fit for a role. It should be clean, scannable, and easy to read in under 30 seconds.

## Essential sections
- Personal details: full name, phone, email, city or location, LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio.
- Career objective or professional summary: a brief statement focused on your value and target role.
- Education: degree, institution, graduation year, CGPA when strong.
- Skills: include technical, professional, and domain-specific strengths.
- Experience: list internships, freelance work, and part-time roles with measurable outcomes.
- Projects: show the problem, tools used, and impact.
- Certifications and achievements: highlight relevant credibility and performance.

## Resume writing tips
- Use simple, ATS-friendly formatting.
- Prefer action verbs like built, developed, optimized, automated, and improved.
- Keep the resume to one page for freshers and one to two pages for experienced candidates.
- Tailor the resume for the job description instead of using a generic template.
- Focus on outcomes, not just responsibilities.
